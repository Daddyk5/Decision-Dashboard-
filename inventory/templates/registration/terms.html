Platform use

Use this system only for authorized Rice Enterprise inventory, fulfillment, customer, and reporting operations. Credentials are personal and every material action is audit logged.

Order fulfillment SLA

Orders are subject to verified stock, agreed delivery windows, credit status, and operational confirmation. The operations team may contact the customer when an order needs review.

Call recording compliance

Agents must obtain and record customer consent before capturing call details, recordings, or follow-up notes. Escalate consent questions to a Team Lead.

Credit limits

Customer orders must remain within the approved credit limit. Finance or an authorized administrator may pause fulfillment for overdue balances.
