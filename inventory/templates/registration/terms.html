Platform use

Use this system only for authorized Rice Enterprise inventory, fulfilment, customer, and reporting operations. Credentials are personal, role-scoped, and subject to operational audit logging.

Order fulfillment SLA

Orders are subject to verified stock, agreed delivery windows, credit status, and operational approval. The operations team may contact the customer when an order requires review.

Call recording compliance

Agents must obtain and document customer consent before capturing call details, recordings, or follow-up notes. Escalate unresolved consent questions to a Team Lead.

Credit limits

Customer orders must remain within the approved credit limit. Finance or an authorized administrator may pause fulfilment for overdue balances or fraud risk.
