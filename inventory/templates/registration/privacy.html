Privacy Policy

Data storage

Operational data is stored in encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL infrastructure and accessed through role-scoped Django services. Secrets are supplied through environment variables.

Privacy and GDPR

We collect only information needed to create accounts, fulfill orders, support customers, and maintain security. Requests for access, correction, or deletion can be sent to the system administrator.

Audit trails

Inventory, order, delivery, payment, and call changes are retained in audit history for operational traceability and compliance.
